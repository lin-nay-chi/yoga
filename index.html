SMALL MOMENTS, EVERY DAY

Less rushing.
More being.

Between classes, before a busy day, or when it’s time to switch off. Start with a few minutes that belong to you.

Make room for yourself. ↗
01Gentle beginningsA 15-minute introduction+

Settle into a comfortable seat for 3 minutes. Spend 8 minutes exploring gentle shoulder rolls, seated side stretches, and easy hands-and-knees movements. Finish with 4 minutes of quiet rest.

02Rise & flowA 10-minute morning pause+

Stand comfortably and breathe naturally for 2 minutes. Take 6 minutes to reach your arms up, lower them slowly, and try gentle side bends. Finish with 2 minutes of stillness, noticing how you feel.

03The quiet momentA 5-minute evening reset+

Sit or lie in a comfortable position. Let your shoulders relax and breathe at your natural pace. Notice the support beneath you. Give yourself five minutes with nowhere else to be.

Move within a comfortable range and pause if something hurts.
